Why it is built this way

Brass on near-black is the actual brand. The accent is #BE9247 — a struck, desaturated metal, chosen precisely because it is not the emerald-and-purple every generated interface arrives in. The signature uses it once, on the rule and the call to action, and nowhere else.

System fonts, not the site's. Archivo and Instrument Sans are the site's faces and no email client will load them. A web font in a signature falls back silently and unevenly, so this uses the system stack deliberately and keeps the identity in the colour and the layout.

A table, not a flexbox. Outlook renders through Word. Divs and modern CSS collapse; a table with inline styles is the only layout that survives Gmail, Outlook, Apple Mail and every mobile client at once.

The image is hosted, not attached. An attached logo shows up as a paperclip on every message you send and gets stripped by some filters. This one loads from the site.

No social icons. The audit finding on our own pages was "no links to your social profiles", and the honest answer is that I do not know which accounts are active. Adding icons that point at empty profiles is worse than none — tell me the live ones and I will add them.
