# MarketWar OS — User Manual

How to use the platform, in plain language. Every dashboard page also has a
floating **Guide** button (bottom-right) with these steps in-context.

---

## Getting started (5 minutes)

1. **Sign up / log in** at marketwaros.com.
2. **Add your brand** in the top-left switcher: name, website, logo, colours,
   product, audience, location. Almost every engine reads this, so fill it in —
   the more real detail, the better the output.
3. **Import your contacts** (Customer Vault) — this is the fuel for most engines.
4. Open any page and hit **Guide** if you're unsure what to do.

Readiness badges you'll see on the Guide panel:
- 🟢 **Ready now** — works with no setup.
- 🔵 **Works now, fuller with a key** — usable immediately; a provider key turns
  the demo into real external effects.
- 🟡 **Shows estimates** — modelled numbers until a data source is connected.

## Core workflows — "I want to…"

### …turn a contact list into customers
1. **Customer Vault** → Upload CSV or Paste rows (any columns auto-detected).
2. Every contact is scored instantly (LTV, churn, intent).
3. Company-only list? Click **Find emails** → gets a real email per company.
4. Use **Email** / **WhatsApp** on any row to contact them in one click.
5. **Segments** groups them; **Recovery** finds win-backs; **Autopilot** can run
   a daily cycle for you.

### …create ads / creatives
1. **Studio (VisualStrike)** → confirm logo + product photo, set headline/offer/CTA.
2. Generate → pick a variant → **Download** it, or **Publish**.
3. **Post it yourself** (in the publish panel) works with zero setup: copy
   caption → download image → open each network's composer.

### …publish to social
1. **Publish Center** → connect **Facebook & Instagram** (Connect with Facebook,
   or paste a Page token). Connect other channels via Zernio.
2. Write the post, pick channels, **Publish** — or use **Post it yourself**.

### …build a landing page
1. **Landing Builder** → fill Business + Objective; paste your Offer.
2. Optionally add a **CTA button text** and a **Product / CTA link** (your
   checkout/booking URL).
3. Generate → **Publish live page** → you get a real shareable `/b/…` URL. Form
   submissions land in the Vault.

### …send email
1. **Email Center** → pick a segment (or a status segment for imported prospects).
2. Choose a **template** (build reusable ones in Email Templates), set the message.
3. **Send** — it filters non-consented/invalid contacts and delivers through your
   own sending infrastructure.

### …find new B2B prospects
1. **LeadWar Room (Prospecting)** → enter product, target industry, deal size, pain.
2. Build the ICP → discover prospects (real companies via live Google).
3. Each gets a Deal Probability score; save the good ones, export or push to Vault.

### …win organic / SEO customers
1. **Organic** / **OMNIRANK** / **Search Dominance** → your real Search Console
   rankings appear once Google is connected (Go-Live → Connect Google).
2. **Website Intel** → enter a URL → **Live site crawl** gives a real measured
   audit (SEO/technical/mobile/social).
3. **SEO Blog Studio** (admin) drafts posts; the **SEO Workbench** generates
   copy-paste schema, llms.txt and meta tags from your brand.

### …make video
1. **AI Video Creator** → write a brief or record your screen in-browser.
2. Trim/clip in the editor (works with no key). Render to MP4 or paste a video URL.
3. Publish, or download and post yourself.

### …get your first customer
1. **First Customer** → a guided flow: finds leads (Serper), drafts outreach,
   and can take payment (Stripe). Follow each step.

### …run strategy / campaigns
- **Strategy**, **Campaign Builder**, **Offer Forge**, **Marketing Warfare**,
  **AI Agents** — fill the brief fields, generate, then execute the pieces via
  Publish / Email / WhatsApp. (Real generation with an AI provider key.)

## Money & admin

- **Billing** — your plan + ACU wallet; top-ups open a real Stripe checkout.
- **Revenue / Money Ledger** — real attributed revenue + ROI from your ledger.
- **Go-Live** (admin) — every money/data switch, green/amber/red, with the exact
  fix. Also holds **Connect Google**.
- **Settings** — brand details + how much **Autopilot** may do on its own.
- **Integrations** — what's connected and each connector's ACU cost.
- **Admin** (owner) — real margins over the ACU ledger.

---

## Honesty guarantees (why numbers you see are trustworthy)

- The OS **never fabricates** ratings, reviews, prices, rankings or emails. What
  it doesn't have, it omits or labels as an estimate.
- Modules showing 🟡 estimates say so, and fill with real figures once a data
  source (Search Console, analytics, ads) is connected.
- Publishing carries an AI-content watermark and passes a compliance gate.
