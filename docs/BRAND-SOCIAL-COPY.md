# Social profiles — the copy, and what it is not allowed to say

Assets: `public/brand/social/` (`npm run social` rebuilds them).

## The rule this copy is bound by

**No social proof that does not exist.** Customers acquired is currently zero, so
there is no "trusted by 500 businesses", no "join thousands", no star rating and
no logo wall. Every one of those is available to write and every one of them is a
lie today — and the entire argument of this product is that its numbers are real.
A first customer who discovers the first claim was invented has no reason to
believe the second.

What replaces it: **specifics**. "Twelve tools in one subscription" and "a free
audit that reads your actual page" are checkable in ninety seconds, which is a
stronger opening than a number nobody can verify.

## What NOT to publish until it is true

Keep this list. Each becomes usable the moment it is earned, and not before.

- Any customer count, revenue figure, or "businesses served".
- Reviews, ratings, or testimonials.
- Competitor comparisons with prices — no competitor's pricing here has been
  verified, and an invented one is both wrong and actionable.
- Case studies. AxionOS and VeryX are the owner's own brands; presenting them as
  independent customer results would be misleading.
- "Trusted by", "used by", "loved by" in any form.
