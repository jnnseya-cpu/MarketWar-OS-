## 0. Read this before you spend anything

**One recommendation, then it is your call.** You asked for Awareness. For a
product nobody has heard of, with zero customers, the pure **Awareness**
objective optimises for *ad recall lift* — it buys eyeballs, teaches you almost
nothing, and builds a weak retargeting pool. **Traffic**, optimised for *landing
page views*, costs slightly more per person and gives you three things Awareness
does not: people actually on the site, a pixel audience worth retargeting, and a
real signal about which message works.

**Recommended:** run the Traffic version below. Both are written out; the ad copy
is identical, only the objective and optimisation change.

**Your pixel will undercount, and you must know this before you judge results.**
The Meta Pixel on marketwaros.com loads *after* a visitor accepts cookies (it is
a UK site and PECR requires consent before tracking). Expect Meta to report
**fewer landing page views than reality** — anyone who ignores the cookie banner
is invisible to it. Judge the campaign on **link clicks and CPC** from Meta, and
on the **audit submissions** you can count yourself. Do not panic when Meta's
landing-page-view number looks low against clicks; that gap is the banner, not a
broken pixel.

**Do not use these words in the ads:** no customer counts, no "trusted by", no
ratings, no testimonials, no competitor names or prices. You have none of them
yet, and the entire product argument is that its numbers are real. One invented
claim in an ad undoes it.

```
AS2 (Founders):
https://www.marketwaros.com/audit?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=paid&utm_campaign=awr-2026q3-manchester&utm_content=founders

AS3 (E-commerce):
https://www.marketwaros.com/audit?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=paid&utm_campaign=awr-2026q3-manchester&utm_content=ecom
```

**Send them to `/audit`, not the home page.** The audit gives something true and
specific before asking for anything, and it needs no account. A cold visitor on
the home page has to decide whether to trust you; on the audit page they get
proof in ninety seconds. If you would rather test the home page, run it as a
fourth ad rather than replacing this.

**URL parameters field** (Meta will append it for you):
```
utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=paid&utm_campaign=awr-2026q3-manchester&utm_content={{ad.name}}&utm_term={{adset.name}}
```

## 5. Creative briefs — the pictures you will make

**Format:** 1:1 (1080×1080) for feed, plus 9:16 (1080×1920) for Stories/Reels.
Make both — Advantage+ placements will use them.

**Brand palette:** deep navy `#101f38` · gold `#c9992e` · off-white `#f4f6f9`.
Logo files are in `public/brand/`. Keep the logo small; this is not a logo ad.

| Ad | The picture | Text on image (Meta favours less) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 — The brake | A large red STOP / emergency-stop button on navy. Blunt, physical, unmistakable. | `STOP` + small: `Halts every campaign` |
| 2 — Twelve bills | A phone screenshot mock of a bank statement with 7 subscription lines highlighted in gold, one crossed out. | `7 bills → 1` |
| 3 — The audit | The audit result UI: a big score (e.g. `62/100`) with two or three finding lines beneath. | `Your site. Right now.` |
| 4 — Not ChatGPT | Split frame: left a bare chat box, right a dashboard of joined-up panels. | `Post` vs `System` |
| 5 — Record yourself | A screen recording with a presenter bubble in the corner, mid-drag, with a motion arrow. | `Both. One file.` |

**Rules for every image**
- Keep text **under ~20% of the image**. Meta no longer hard-rejects for it, but
  heavy-text creative still gets suppressed reach.
- Show a **real screen** wherever you can. A screenshot of the actual product
  outperforms an illustration for a product nobody has heard of.
- **No stock photos of people in suits shaking hands.** Your buyer is a plumber
  in a van or a founder at a kitchen table. If you use a person, use that person.
- **No fake dashboards with invented numbers.** Screenshot the real audit output.
  If a number appears in an image, it must be a number the product actually
  produced.

## 6. Set these up on day one

**Custom audiences to create now** (they need time to fill before you can retarget):

```
1. "MW - Site visitors 180d"       → Website · All visitors · 180 days
2. "MW - Audit page 180d"          → Website · People who visited /audit · 180 days
3. "MW - Video 50% 365d"           → Video · watched 50% · 365 days  (once you run video)
4. "MW - Page engagers 365d"       → Facebook Page · anyone who engaged · 365 days
5. "MW - IG engagers 365d"         → Instagram account · anyone who engaged · 365 days
```

Audiences 4 and 5 fill **even with no site traffic**, which is why they go in on
day one. Retargeting is the next campaign; this one exists to fill these.

## 7. What to judge it on

| Metric | Where | What "fine" looks like at this stage |
|---|---|---|
| CPM | Meta | £4–£12 UK small-business targeting |
| CTR (link) | Meta | Above 1.0% — below 0.7% the creative is the problem |
| CPC (link) | Meta | £0.40–£1.50 |
| Landing page views | Meta | **Will undercount — see §0** |
| **Audit submissions** | Your own `/dashboard/acquisition` | **The only number that matters** |

**The one number:** an audit submission is a named person who typed their own
website in and asked for the report. Everything else is traffic. The GTM plan's
whole thesis is that customers acquired is currently zero — this campaign's job
is to move *that*, not to buy impressions.

**Kill rules, decided now so you don't argue with yourself later:**
- An **ad** with under 0.5% CTR after 1,000 impressions → turn off.
- An **ad set** with no audit submission after £150 → turn off, move budget to
  the best performer.
- Do **not** touch anything in the first 72 hours. Editing resets the learning
  phase and you pay for it twice.

---

## 8. Compliance, briefly

- **Special ad category:** None. You are not advertising credit, employment,
  housing or politics.
- **No personal attributes.** Meta rejects copy that implies you know something
  about the viewer. Write *"Been burnt by a marketing company before?"* (a
  question) — never *"You've been burnt by a marketing company."*
- **Claims must be true.** "Twelve tools", "£19 a month", "free audit, no card"
  are all verifiable on the page. Keep it that way.
- **Cookie consent already handled.** The site's banner loads before any tracker,
  which is what keeps the pixel lawful in the UK. Do not remove it to improve
  measurement.
