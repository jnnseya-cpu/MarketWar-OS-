**Honesty constraint (owner directive, overrides ranking promises):** Google and
other search engines do not allow anyone to guarantee permanent first-page or
number-one rankings. Even technically compliant pages are not guaranteed to be
crawled, indexed or served, and irresponsible ranking tactics can damage a
website’s visibility and reputation. MarketWar should therefore promise
continuous competitive optimisation, maximum eligible visibility and measurable
organic revenue growth, not an impossible guaranteed ranking.

## MARKETWAR DYNAMIC SEARCH DOMINANCE ENGINE
Autonomous SEO, AI Discovery, Social Search and Organic Revenue Command System

### 1. Core Mission
Make every connected brand: easier to discover, understand, trust, recommend;
more authoritative than competitors; more visible wherever customers search; more
capable of turning organic visibility into revenue. The objective is not simply
to increase rankings, but: “To make the user’s business the strongest, clearest,
most useful and most commercially relevant answer wherever customers search,
compare, ask or buy.” Operates across Google Search/Images/Lens/Maps/Shopping/
News/Discover, Bing, Copilot, Yahoo, YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook,
LinkedIn, Reddit, Pinterest, app stores, marketplaces, AI assistants, answer
engines, voice search, local discovery platforms.

### 2. Positioning
Not a keyword tool — “The autonomous organic customer-acquisition engine that
discovers demand, builds authority, improves websites, creates winning content,
earns trusted citations and converts search visibility into revenue.” Promise:
“Be found first. Be trusted faster. Be recommended more. Convert the demand.”
“Competitors chase keywords. MarketWar captures entire customer decisions.”

### 3. One Dynamic Agent — the Dynamic SEO Commander Agent
Moves across authorised website infrastructure (homepage, product/service/
category/location pages, blog, help centre, FAQs, comparison, landing pages,
images, videos, metadata, internal links, structured data, sitemaps, redirects,
canonicals, navigation, conversion elements, CMS, Search Console, Analytics,
merchant feeds, local profiles). Operating modes: **Observe** (analyse only),
**Recommend** (propose for approval), **Assisted** (draft + user approves),
**Autonomous** (implement pre-approved low-risk changes within controls),
**Emergency Recovery** (pause harmful deploys, restore metadata, repair
indexation, roll back).

### 4. Operating Loop
Discover Demand → Analyse Search Intent → Inspect Competitors → Audit Website
Eligibility → Prioritise Revenue Opportunities → Create/Improve the Best Answer →
Strengthen Internal Authority → Earn External Authority → Submit and Validate →
Distribute Across Search and Social → Measure Rankings, Citations and Revenue →
Learn, Refresh and Defend → Repeat Continuously. SEO is a permanent competitive
operation, never a one-off project.

### 5. Day-One Search Domination Scan (Search Revenue Audit)
Step 1 crawl & understand (business type, products, services, locations,
segments, topics, pages, intent covered/missing, internal-link structure,
technical barriers, conversion pathways, content quality, brand entities,
commercial priorities). Step 2 connect source-of-truth (Search Console,
Analytics, Bing Webmaster, CMS, ecommerce, CRM, Google Business Profile, Merchant
Centre, YouTube, social, reviews). Step 3 find immediate search losses (unindexed
valuable pages, wrong noindex, blocked resources, broken canonicals, duplicates,
orphans, missing structured data, slow product pages, weak titles, thin
commercial pages, unclear entities, missing location pages, poor internal
linking, traffic on non-converting pages, high-impression low-click queries,
ranking pages with weak CTAs, queries just outside page one). Step 4 build the
Search Money Map (Immediate Wins, Ranking Opportunities, Content Gaps, Authority
Gaps, Competitor Displacement, AI Recommendation Opportunities).

### 6. Search Opportunity Scoring
Score = Search Demand × Purchase Intent × Conversion Probability × Customer
Lifetime Value × Ranking Feasibility × Authority Potential × Strategic Importance
÷ Competition ÷ Cost ÷ Time to Impact. Each card shows target query/topic, intent,
funnel stage, demand, current position, competitor strength, content/technical/
authority work required, expected traffic/leads/revenue/cost, confidence,
recommended next action. Prioritise revenue, not search volume alone.

### 7. Search Intent Intelligence
Intent categories: informational, commercial investigation, transactional,
navigational, local, comparison, problem-aware, solution-aware, brand-aware,
urgent purchase, repeat purchase, support, reputation verification. Commercial
signals: best, price, cost, buy, book, near me, available today, alternative,
comparison, review, supplier, quote, demo, trial, discount, delivery,
installation. Create different page/content strategies per intent.

### 8. Keyword, Topic and Prompt Universe
Unified discovery universe: keywords, long-tail, questions, problems, product/
service/local/comparison/branded/competitor searches, voice phrases, AI prompts,
social phrases, hashtags, community language, industry terms, objections.
Clustering into pillar/supporting/commercial/product/local/comparison/FAQ/
troubleshooting/thought-leadership. Each cluster: one primary authority page,
supporting content, internal-link plan, conversion pathway, structured-data plan,
refresh schedule, authority-acquisition plan.

### 9. Competitor Search War Room
Continuously compare vs search competitors (ranking/new/lost pages, features,
rich results, AI citations, backlinks, linking domains, updates, speed, structured
data, internal links, product info, pricing, reviews, video, social). Detect
weaknesses (outdated pages, weak explanations, missing evidence, poor product
detail, weak local relevance, missing FAQs, slow pages, broken links, thin
comparisons, weak trust, poor visuals, intent mismatch, negative reviews, no
expert contribution). One-click responses (stronger alternative page, comparison
page, expand topic, better product guide, local landing page, add evidence, demo
video, digital PR, strengthen internal links, improve conversion). Compete through
usefulness, originality, trust, authority, execution — never spam/manipulation/
sabotage.

### 10. Technical SEO Autonomous Engineer
Continuously protect crawl/render/index/understand eligibility. Checks: HTTP
status, DNS, server errors, crawl/render failures, robots, noindex, canonicals,
redirect chains/loops, duplicates, soft 404s, broken links, orphans, pagination,
faceted nav, JS/mobile rendering, URL consistency, HTTPS, sitemap accuracy, crawl
depth, index bloat, Core Web Vitals, structured-data validity. Repairs (with
approval): canonicals, redirect chains, sitemap cleanup, broken internal links,
image dimensions, server-rendered metadata, crawlable anchors, duplicate
consolidation, segmented sitemaps, submit changed URLs, repair accidental
noindex. robots.txt is crawl control, not the primary way to keep a page out of
Google — use noindex/access controls.

### 11. Intelligent Sitemap and Indexing Engine
Dynamic sitemaps by type (main, products, categories, services, locations,
articles, videos, images, news, languages). Include only canonical indexable
URLs; remove redirects/errors; accurate lastmod; segment large maps; submit;
monitor processing; alert on unindexed submissions. Sitemaps are discovery
signals, not guarantees. IndexNow on create/update/delete/redirect (still no
indexing/ranking guarantee).

### 12. Structured Data Intelligence Engine
Recommend/generate/validate/maintain schema (Organisation, LocalBusiness,
Product, Offer, AggregateRating, Review, Article, NewsArticle, BreadcrumbList,
Event, JobPosting, Course, VideoObject, Recipe, SoftwareApplication, Dataset,
MerchantReturnPolicy, ShippingDetails). JSON-LD recommended; valid markup does not
guarantee rich results. Safeguards: schema reflects visible content; never fake
reviews/ratings/prices/stock/unavailable offers; validate before publish; monitor
errors; remove obsolete; entity consistency.

### 13. Dynamic On-Page Optimisation Agent
Improve titles, descriptions, headings, intro, intent alignment, entity clarity,
product/service details, FAQs, evidence, examples, visuals, internal links, CTAs,
trust, author info, update date, related content, accessibility — using live
data. Rules: high-impression/low-click → improve title/description/positioning;
good traffic/weak conversion → improve offer/CTA/proof/structure/capture/speed/
pricing; ranking decline → investigate competitors/outdated info/lost links/
indexing/technical/intent shift/cannibalisation; multiple pages one topic →
consolidate/canonicalise/redirect/differentiate/internal-link.

### 14. Content Superiority Engine
No mass filler; distinctive value only (original research, first-party data,
expert explanation, demo, real questions, case study, template, calculator,
comparison, visual guide, local knowledge, current pricing, original photos,
video, process, evidence). Score for intent/completeness/accuracy/originality/
expertise/evidence/readability/entity clarity/visual usefulness/conversion/
freshness/differentiation. Below threshold → not auto-published.

### 15. Search-to-Revenue Content Factory
Every item maps to revenue: demand discovery, commercial investigation,
transactional, local conversion, product/service, competitor alternative, trust,
customer-support, AI citation content. Required fields: target query, audience,
intent, funnel stage, offer, CTA, conversion event, revenue objective,
attribution rule, refresh date.

### 16. Dynamic Internal Authority Engine
Strategic internal links. Find orphans; identify weak-authority pages; recommend
hubs; add contextual links; improve anchors; remove misleading links; prevent
repetitive linking; strengthen commercial pages; repair broken links; reduce
crawl depth. Map: strongest hubs, weakest commercial pages, orphans,
cannibalisation, depth, clusters, link flow, revenue contribution.

### 17. Ethical Backlink and Digital Authority Engine
Build the strongest relevant authority profile (not the largest count). Methods:
original research, statistics, expert commentary, digital PR, journalist
requests, partner/supplier pages, associations, local citations, universities,
events, podcasts, resource pages, broken-link replacement, product reviews, case
studies, free tools, templates, data viz. Authority Opportunity = Domain
Relevance × Editorial Quality × Audience Value × Trust × Referral Potential ×
Ranking Impact ÷ Acquisition Cost ÷ Risk. Prohibited: paid-link networks, comment
spam, hidden links, hacking, injection, PBN abuse, mass irrelevant guest posts,
fake scholarships, manipulative redirects, negative SEO. Protect the user from
penalty/reputation risk.

### 18. AI Search and Recommendation Engine
Optimise eligibility for Google AI Overviews/AI Mode, Copilot, ChatGPT search,
Gemini, Claude, Perplexity, other answer engines. Grounded in search
fundamentals: accessible pages, useful content, accurate information — no separate
guaranteed AI-ranking mechanism. AI Visibility Agent: build a prompt universe,
test customer questions, record mentions/citations/competitor recommendations,
analyse why competitors are selected, identify missing facts, improve entity
clarity, strengthen evidence, create concise answer sections, improve product
facts, maintain consistent info, monitor over time. AI Recommendation Readiness
Score: entity clarity, topical authority, citation availability, product-data
quality, brand consistency, external corroboration, review credibility, answer
completeness, freshness, trust, geographic relevance. Distinguish being mentioned
vs cited vs positively recommended vs accurately represented vs producing
attributable traffic/revenue.

### 19. Brand Entity and Knowledge Engine
One consistent brand truth layer. Entity record: legal name, trading name,
description, founders, locations, contact info, products, services, categories,
prices, service areas, social accounts, policies, awards, certifications, trusted
references. Consistency monitoring: detect conflicting information across website,
local profiles, social platforms, directories, review sites, merchant feeds,
knowledge panels, partner sites, press coverage. Recommend corrections where
information is outdated or inconsistent.

### 20. Local Search Domination Engine
For location-dependent businesses, manage: location pages, service-area pages,
local schema, business-profile accuracy, opening hours, photos, product listings,
service listings, review velocity, review responses, local citations, local
backlinks, local content, map visibility, direction and call conversions. Local
opportunity cards, e.g. “High demand for this service within five miles”, “Two
competitors have weaker review coverage”, “This location page lacks service
detail”, “Weekend opening hours are inconsistent”, “Create a page for this
underserved district.”

### 21. Ecommerce Search Growth Engine
For ecommerce: product-feed optimisation, product titles, descriptions, category
architecture, variant management, availability, price consistency, product schema,
merchant listings, image optimisation, review integrity, filter-page control,
product comparison, out-of-stock handling, related products, cross-sell links,
seasonal pages. Connect search visibility to product views, add-to-cart rate,
checkout rate, revenue, margin, stock availability, returns.

### 22. Image and Visual Search Engine
Connect the image-generation gateway to SEO: create original product images,
context-rich lifestyle images; compress; responsive dimensions; descriptive alt
text; captions; image sitemaps where suitable; product metadata; preserve image
quality; avoid misleading generated imagery; keep exact product and logo
information. Google Lens readiness — images clearly show product, brand, variants,
relevant context, accurate colour, packaging, distinguishing details.

### 23. Video and YouTube Search Engine
Transform high-value topics into YouTube videos, Shorts, tutorials,
demonstrations, comparisons, FAQs, testimonials (with consent), product
explainers, local videos. Video SEO functions: topic selection, search-title
generation, description, chapters, captions, transcript, thumbnail testing, video
structured data, embedding recommendations, related article generation, CTA
tracking, clip repurposing.
